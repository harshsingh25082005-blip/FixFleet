In a city, a small problem at home can quickly become stressful. A leaking tap, a faulty switchboard, a broken fan, or an air conditioner that stops working can disturb daily life. At such times, people often struggle to find a reliable plumber, electrician, carpenter, appliance technician, or other handyman. They usually depend on neighbours, friends, local shops, or random internet searches. This process takes time, and there is often no guarantee about the person's skill, availability, behaviour, or service charge.



FixFleet is designed to make finding local home-service professionals simple, quick, and trustworthy. It is a mobile or web application where residents can search for the service they need, such as plumbing, electrical repair, carpentry, painting, cleaning, or appliance servicing. They can view the profiles of nearby service providers, including their skills, experience, ratings, charges, service area, and available time slots. After choosing a suitable professional, the customer can book a visit, make a payment, and receive updates about the booking.



The system also helps technicians and handymen find work in an organised way. A service provider can register, create a profile, list their skills and working hours, and receive service requests from customers. They can accept or reject a request depending on their availability and update the status after the work is completed. This helps them manage their jobs better and reach more customers without depending only on word-of-mouth.



An administrator manages FixFleet by verifying service-provider profiles, monitoring bookings, handling complaints, and maintaining user records. The system also uses payment and notification services to make payments secure and keep customers informed through booking confirmations, reminders, and status updates.



The main goal of FixFleet is to reduce the stress of finding dependable local help during urgent or everyday home problems. It creates a safer and more transparent connection between residents who need assistance and skilled workers who are looking for service opportunities.
